# Phase 2B route and tools roadmap

These items are documented for a later phase and must not be added to the
public navigation until each destination has real, reviewed content and a
working route.

## Future navigation destinations

- Industries
- Tools
- About
- Terms
- Editorial Policy
- Correction Policy
- AI-Assisted Content Disclosure
- Affiliate Disclosure

## Marketing calculators

Future calculators may cover CPL, CPA, ROAS and planning scenarios. Each tool
must use deterministic, documented formulas; explain its inputs and
limitations; provide a useful result without collecting unnecessary personal
data; and pass accessibility, responsive, SEO and production-build checks.

Until those tools exist, the homepage points visitors to currently available
guides, checklists and resources. It must not expose a `/tools` destination or
present an unfinished calculator as active.
