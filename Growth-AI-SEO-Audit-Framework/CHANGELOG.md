## How to Contribute to Future Editions

Practitioners using this framework are encouraged to document:

1. Checkpoints where criteria proved difficult to apply in practice
2. New signal types observed in audit work that are not covered by existing checkpoints
3. Changes in tool availability that affect checkpoint procedures
4. Evidence of checkpoint scoring that does not correlate with expected outcomes

Submit observations to the framework maintainer at Technocrats Digimate for review and inclusion in future editions.
